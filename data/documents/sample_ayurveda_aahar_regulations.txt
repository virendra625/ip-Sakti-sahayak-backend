Regulation 3: Definition and Scope of Ayurveda Aahar
"Ayurveda Aahar" means food prepared in accordance with the recipes or books/processes specified in the First Schedule of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and other recognized authoritative Ayurvedic publications. It includes foods containing ingredients specified in Schedule A of these regulations.

Clause 3(2): Exclusion from Ayurveda Aahar
Ayurveda Aahar shall NOT include Ayurvedic medicines, proprietary Ayurvedic drugs, phytopharmaceuticals, or products containing synthetic vitamins, added synthetic minerals, synthetic chemicals, or purified active pharmaceutical isolates.

Regulation 5: Labelling and Packaging Standards
1. Every package of Ayurveda Aahar shall carry the designated official "Ayurveda Aahar" logo.
2. The label must explicitly state: "For health and dietary consumption — NOT for medicinal use or treatment of disease."
3. The advisory must indicate the target consumer category, recommended daily dosage, and contra-indications where applicable.
